Using Guardian

This release is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Use it only for your own safety and with the consent of the people involved. Do not use it for hidden tracking, harassment, impersonation, or unauthorized access to another person's location or account.

Guardian is a tool for contacting trusted people. It does not replace emergency services or guarantee delivery, monitoring, response, or continuous location updates. Test Mode simulates alerts and does not send real notifications. Features marked unavailable remain unavailable even if your account has a subscription.

Account access

Supported versions offer Sign in with Apple on iOS and Sign in with Google on iOS and Android. Keep using the same sign-in provider and account to access your existing Guardian account. Separate Apple and Google identities are not automatically merged. A forwarded invitation link does not by itself establish a trusted account connection; the inviting user must confirm a social-sign-in recipient.

15 days without a payment method

A new eligible Guardian account receives 15 days of access from its recorded account start date. No store subscription or payment method is required for this account trial, and it does not automatically become a paid subscription. Deleting and recreating the same identity does not restart this trial.

Monthly store subscription and introductory offer

Where subscriptions are available, eligible new subscribers may receive one calendar month free by confirming the monthly subscription in Apple App Store or Google Play checkout. The store determines offer eligibility. This is a separate store offer, not a promise of an additional month for merely adding a card. Guardian does not receive your card number or inspect whether your store account has a card.

Before confirming, review the price, currency, free-trial duration, renewal interval and next charge shown by the store. The subscription automatically renews at the displayed monthly price unless canceled according to the store's rules. After an introductory free period, the displayed monthly price applies. The final store checkout controls eligibility, taxes, price and charge dates.

Cancel, restore or request a refund

Use Account → Manage subscription in Guardian or open Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. Cancel before the store's stated renewal deadline to avoid a further charge. The store determines access and refund eligibility after cancellation; your statutory consumer rights are unaffected.

Use Restore purchases while signed into the same Guardian account and the store account used for purchase. Store purchases cannot be transferred to an unrelated Guardian account. If a payment or restoration is pending, wait for confirmation rather than purchasing again.

Deleting the app or your Guardian account does not cancel the store subscription. Cancel it separately in your store account. Guardian does not charge a subscription unless you approve the store checkout.
