LiVo GUARDIAN

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 4 September 2026

This policy explains how LiVo Guardian, published by Mehdi Rahbar under the LiVo name (“LiVo”, “we”, “us”), handles information in the LiVo Guardian mobile app and its trusted-contact web views. Contact: goodfortune.app.ss@gmail.com.

Safety boundary: LiVo Guardian is a trusted-contact safety tool, not an emergency service. It does not automatically contact emergency services and cannot guarantee delivery. Contact the appropriate local emergency service directly whenever you can.

1. Who may use this release

The initial public release is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children and is not a hidden child-tracking service. A child/family edition would require separate parent or guardian consent, age assurance, safeguarding controls, and legal review.

LiVo Guardian does not request access to your phone address book. Trusted-contact details are entered deliberately inside the app. It does not use advertising identifiers, sell personal information, or use personal information for cross-app advertising or tracking.

3. Location in the background

With your device permission, LiVo Guardian can collect and transmit location in the background—when the app is closed or not in use—only while a safety alert you started is active or while you have separately enabled ongoing last-known-location sharing for a fully trusted accepted contact. Selected recipients can see this location until the alert/share ends or access is revoked. You can stop sharing and delete the latest stored trusted-location point in the app. Operating-system and network conditions can interrupt updates.

4. Regional statistics choice

The current version presents regional statistics as enabled by default and provides a visible Settings checkbox to turn them off. A real, non-Test alert with an initial location may then increment only a coarse regional/time counter. The counter contains no account, device, contact, group or incident identifier, phone number, name, raw message or precise coordinate. We do not currently send these counters directly to police or city authorities and would require separate legal review and a formal agreement before such use.

This feature must obtain any affirmative consent required by the platform and applicable law before collection. You can withdraw at any time in Settings; later alerts will not contribute after the server records the change.

5. When information is shared

  • People you select: alert, display name, location and status are shared only with the trusted contacts selected for that alert or separately trusted share.
  • Service processors: Cloudflare may process encrypted application state and network traffic. Expo and Apple/Google push services may process push tokens and notification payloads when production push is enabled. Contracted identity or message providers, if enabled, process only data needed for that service.
  • User-approved SMS: the operating-system messaging app and carrier process recipients, text and location link only after the user reviews the composer and presses Send.
  • Legal/safety: we may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety, subject to applicable law.

Processors must provide protections consistent with this policy and contractual requirements. International transfers, if any, require an appropriate legal mechanism and safeguards.

6. Retention

  • Incident event history: up to 30 days by the current service policy.
  • Resolved incident records: up to 90 days by the current service policy.
  • Consent and security audit records: up to 365 days, unless law requires a different period.
  • Trusted ongoing share: only the latest location is retained, for no more than 30 days from server receipt; revoked/ineligible/expired points are not served.
  • Coarse regional counters: up to 90 days from the start of their six-hour bucket.
  • Push and device credentials: until rotation, logout, revocation or account deletion as applicable.
  • Account deletion: access is revoked immediately and bounded erasure is targeted within 30 days, except narrowly required legal/security records.

7. Your controls and rights

Inside LiVo Guardian you can change your display name, export account data, review retention, revoke a device or contact, leave/revoke memberships, revoke or rotate invitations and share links, stop trusted-location sharing, delete its latest point, turn off regional statistics, and request account deletion. You may also use the external deletion page. Depending on your location, you may have additional access, correction, objection, restriction and portability rights.

8. Security

Production connections use HTTPS. Sensitive application state is encrypted at the application layer before storage, tokens are hashed or protected where designed, and access is account/device scoped. No system is perfectly secure; keep your phone and account protected and revoke a lost device promptly.

9. Permissions and choices

System permission prompts and in-app controls are separate. You may decline notification or location permissions, although the related safety feature will not work. Urgent sound is opt-in and may still be suppressed by silent/Focus/DND, volume, battery/OEM policy, connectivity, or a powered-off phone. LiVo does not read the device passcode, turn on connectivity, or guarantee hardware-button triggers.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service or law changes. Material changes will be disclosed in the app or on this page before they take effect where required. Questions or privacy requests: goodfortune.app.ss@gmail.com.
