Fastest option: delete inside the app

  1. Open LiVo Guardian and sign in.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Under Privacy & Data, enter DELETE and choose Delete account.

The app immediately revokes access, removes active sharing/credentials and starts bounded erasure. The current service target is completion within 30 days.
