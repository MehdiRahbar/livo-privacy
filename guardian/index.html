LiVo GUARDIAN

Support and safety information

LiVo Guardian helps adults reach selected trusted people with a clear help level and location information.

Not an emergency service

LiVo Guardian does not automatically contact emergency services and cannot guarantee internet, push, or SMS delivery. If you can, contact the appropriate local emergency service directly.

Get help with the app

Email goodfortune.app.ss@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, or precise location unless support explicitly requests it through a verified secure channel.

Notification and location checks

Open LiVo Guardian Settings to review notification permission, urgent-sound choice, location access, trusted-location shares, regional statistics, and signed-in devices. Phone settings, silent/Focus/DND, connectivity, battery policy, and a powered-off device can prevent an alert sound or update.
